INGREDIENTS

  • Bell Peppers & Onions: These veggies cook quickly and work perfect for this recipe.
  • Chicken: Boneless skinless chicken breasts sliced into strips are the star of the show.
  • Seasonings: Chili powder, oregano, and ground cumin provide the perfect seasonings for the chicken here.
  • Flour Tortillas: They are the superior tortilla for this recipe because they are easy to fold.
  • Cheese: Everybody knows the best part of a quesadilla is the cheese.
  • Avocado: Added for a creamy, fresh addition to these quesadillas.
  • Scallions: Thinly sliced scallions add character to the tortilla.
  • Sour Cream: For dipping purposes.

STEP-BY-STEP INSTRUCTIONS

  1. Sauté peppers and onions with salt and pepper: Cook them down until soft—around 5 minutes—so that there is no unwanted crunch in the quesadillas. Once the veggies are an ideal texture, transfer them to a plate, and add some vegetable oil to your pan.
  2. Add your strips of chicken to the pan and season: season with your spices and salt and pepper to taste. If these seasonings are not quite enough, feel free to taste and adjust as you go!
  3. Stir Chicken Occasionally: Stir and monitor chicken until cooked golden brown.
  4. Transfer To a Plate: Once the chicken is ready to go, transfer to a plate so you can get started on the vessel (the tortilla, obviously).
  5. Add Tortilla to The Pan and Sprinkle Generous Amounts of Ingredients to Half the Tortilla: Don't go overboard and create a quesadilla explosion.
  6. Fold Other Half of Tortilla Over and Cook Only Flipping Once til Golden: Any spilled cheese has turned into crispy, irresistible frico
  7. Repeat Steps for Other Quesadillas: Slice into wedges, and add all your desired toppings.
